# Services: frequently asked questions

## How long does a full service take?

A full service takes about two hours of work. Most bikes are ready the next working day.

## Do you repair electric bikes?

We repair the mechanical parts of electric bikes, such as brakes, gears, and tires. We do not repair batteries or motors.

## Can I wait while you fix a flat tire?

Yes. A flat tire repair usually takes about 20 minutes, so you are welcome to wait in the shop.

## Do you sell new bikes?

No. We only repair and service bikes, and we sell common spare parts.
